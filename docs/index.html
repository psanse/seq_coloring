How they differ

SEQ goes vertex by vertex (index order) and gives each vertex the smallest color not used by an already colored neighbor.

ISEQ goes color by color: it builds one independent set at a time, repeatedly taking the lowest-index candidate and discarding its neighbors from the candidates. It needs just two bitsets. The two can yield different numbers of colors.
